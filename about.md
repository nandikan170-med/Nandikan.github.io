## My Content Development Approach

I follow a structured approach to developing scientific educational content:

1. **Research:** Review relevant scientific references and source material.
2. **Understand:** Identify the central concepts and learning objectives.
3. **Structure:** Organize information into a logical learning sequence.
4. **Write:** Develop clear explanations and learner-friendly narration.
5. **Visualize:** Plan illustrations, animation scenes, and storyboards where appropriate.
6. **Review:** Verify scientific accuracy, terminology, clarity, and consistency.

The goal is to make scientific information accessible without compromising its accuracy.
